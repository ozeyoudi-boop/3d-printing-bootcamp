3D Printing Bootcamp

Design a model, edit and repair it, convert it, slice it and print it. Eight short modules, a capstone project, quizzes with explanations, and an on-page design-for-print checker. Your progress is saved in this browser.

Start here   Progress: 0%

Who it's for

You own an X2D (or any FDM printer) and want to move from downloading models to making your own. No prior CAD or slicing knowledge needed. You'll need a computer, calipers and filament.

Curriculum

  1. 00Orientation: your printer, your workflow
  2. 01How FDM printing really works
  3. 02File formats and conversion
  4. 03Design your first model
  5. 04Design for print
  6. 05Edit, repair and remix models
  7. 06Slicing in Bambu Studio
  8. 07The X2D, filaments and upkeep
  9. 08Capstone: snap-fit box
  10. 09Reference and troubleshooting

Outcomes

  • Design a parametric part and export it correctly.
  • Repair and remix downloaded models legally and cleanly.
  • Pick settings by purpose, not by habit.
  • Print a snap-fit box that works.
About the printer. The course assumes your “XT2D” is the Bambu Lab X2D. X2D-specific claims are limited to what launch coverage confirms; for everything else, follow the official Bambu Lab Wiki.
