The one-sentence model

A 3D print is a design turned into a mesh file, turned into toolpaths (G-code) by a slicer, then melted plastic laid down layer by layer. Every problem you will ever have lives in one of those three steps, and knowing which one is 80% of fixing it.

Your printer

You wrote “Pampolab XT2D”. This course assumes that means the Bambu Lab X2D, launched in April 2026. If your printer is a different model, modules 1–6 still apply to any FDM printer; only module 7 is X2D-specific.

Confirmed from launch coverage: a dual-nozzle toolhead with a mechanical nozzle-switching mechanism, an enclosed chamber heated up to 65 °C, nozzles up to 300 °C, about 256 × 256 × 256–260 mm build volume with the main nozzle (less X travel and height when the second nozzle is involved), and the Bambu Studio + MakerWorld ecosystem. For anything else, such as exact profile names, AMS compatibility lists or maintenance intervals, read the official Bambu Lab Wiki for your printer. This course does not guess.

The six-step workflow

StepWhat happensTypical app
1. DesignCreate or find a modelFusion, Tinkercad, FreeCAD, Onshape, MakerWorld
2. Edit / repairFix errors, change dimensions, combine partsBlender, Bambu Studio, your CAD app
3. Export / convertSave as STEP, 3MF or STLSame app, or a script (module 2)
4. SliceOrient, add supports, pick material and qualityBambu Studio
5. PrintSend to the X2D, watch the first layerBambu Studio / Bambu Handy
6. Measure + iterateCheck with calipers, change one thingCalipers + your notes
Habit that makes you good fast: change one thing between prints, write it in your notes box, and measure the result with calipers. Skill in 3D printing is mostly disciplined iteration.

Setup checklist

  • Bambu Studio installed and your printer bound to it.
  • A MakerWorld account (free) for downloading and remixing models.
  • One CAD app: Tinkercad (browser, easiest), Fusion (formerly Fusion 360; check Autodesk's current personal-use terms), Onshape or FreeCAD.
  • Blender (free) for mesh editing.
  • Digital calipers (about 0.01 mm resolution). Essential.
  • A folder structure: models/source (CAD files), models/export (STL/3MF), prints (Bambu project files).

Example: Design = Tinkercad first, Fusion later; Edit = Blender; Export = 3MF from CAD; Slice = Bambu Studio; Print = X2D; Measure = calipers. The point is that every step has one owner, so you never wonder where to fix a problem.
EXERCISE 2

Check your calipers

Close the calipers fully. Do they read 0.00? Measure a coin or a known object twice.

Hint
Zero them with the jaws closed and clean.
Show solution
If the zero drifts, clean the jaws and replace the battery. Trustworthy measurement is the foundation of every tolerance in module 4.
EXERCISE 3

Show solution

Checkpoint quiz

Where do most print problems originate?

Splitting problems into design / slicing / print stages is the core diagnostic skill.

Which app is best for repairing or sculpting a downloaded mesh?

Blender is a mesh editor; parametric CAD apps are best for dimensioned parts.

What should you do between two test prints?

One variable at a time is the only way to learn what caused a change.

What should you trust for exact X2D numbers?

Specs and profiles change with firmware and software releases.
