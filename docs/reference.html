Troubleshooting

SymptomLikely causeTry
First layer won't stickDirty plate, wrong plate setting, wrong first-layer calibrationClean the plate; verify plate type in the slicer; re-run calibration
Corners lifting (warping)Cooling/shrinkage, large footprintBrim, enclosure heat for ABS/ASA, avoid sharp base corners
StringingWet filament, temperature too highDry the filament; reduce temperature; check retraction settings
Rough overhang undersidesOverhang > 45°Reorient, chamfer, or add support
Holes too smallNormal plastic behaviourAdd clearance in CAD
Layers separatedUnder-extrusion or cold hotendCheck filament dryness and temperature; increase wall loops
Supports won't come offSupport interface too dense/closeIncrease the support gap in the slicer; use the support nozzle for supports
Parts won't fit togetherTolerance too tightAdd 0.1 mm clearance and reprint

Glossary

B-rep
Boundary representation: exact solid surfaces used by CAD and STEP.
Brim
A flat ring around the first layer to improve adhesion.
Elephant foot
Bulge at the bottom edge caused by first-layer squish.
G-code
The machine instructions the slicer generates.
Infill
Internal sparse fill pattern.
Manifold
A watertight mesh where each edge belongs to exactly two faces.
Perimeter / wall loop
One outline pass of extruded line.
Slicer
Software that cuts a model into layers and makes toolpaths (Bambu Studio).
Tessellation
Converting curved surfaces into triangles.

Where to go next
